Seawater. Structure. Systems.

Building controlled systems for ocean-grown food and freshwater.

The vision

Most of our planet is ocean. Almost none of it grows food.

We imagine floating, sun-powered platforms that turn seawater into freshwater, and freshwater into harvests, right beside the coastal communities that need them most.

Seawater

The most abundant resource on Earth, made into freshwater with sunlight.

Structure

Modular platforms that shelter controlled growing space on open water.

Systems

Sensing and software that keep water, nutrients, and energy in balance.

We are starting small, on land, and building toward the water.
